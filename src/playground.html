Tuning playground

More Like This for a set of seeds, ranked by the scorer atlas serves, with any of its knobs moved. Every field starts at production's value; only what you change is sent, and every row re-ranks when you stop typing or let go of a slider. Beside each title is its rank in production's row. Points are what each signal added to the score (spread × weight × value). Everything on the page is in the address, so a setup can be shared with Copy link, or saved with Export.
